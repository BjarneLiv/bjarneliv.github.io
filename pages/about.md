Bjarne Liveyns (23, they/them) is an actor, performer, writer from Ghent, Belgium. They're currently (2025) pursuing a Master in Drama at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts (KASK) in Ghent. 
They have a passion for all things acting, voice work, shorts, the stage... and often collaborate with fellow students in film or animation because of this.
Projects that are the result of these collaborations will gradually be added in the projects tab as they roll out.
In their spare time Bjarne enjoys playing jazz tunes on the trumpet and exercising.
