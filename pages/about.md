I'm Bjarne Liveyns, a passionate actor and performer currently pursuing an Academic Bachelor of Drama at the Royal Academy of Fine Arts (KASK) in Ghent, Belgium. With a foundation in Word Art-Drama and years of experience in theater, film, and part-time arts education.

My work spans youth theater, academic performances, and short films, where I thrive on collaboration and storytelling. I'm fluent in Dutch and English, I have a knack for picking up dialects, alongside skills in physical performance techniques like Alexander Technique, among others. When I'm not on stage or in front of the camera, you'll likely find me practicing jazz trumpet or enjoying endurance sports like swimming and cycling.

I'm always eager to connect with others in the performing arts world, so feel free to reach out!
